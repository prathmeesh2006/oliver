SECTION 04 CARAT WEIGHTS • HALLMARKING

Products, Pricing & Gemological Accuracy

Every jewel curated by OLIVER is individually crafted within our Jaipur lapidary ateliers, featuring 100% BIS Hallmarked precious metals (14KT, 18KT, or 22KT Gold) accompanied by individual diamond and gemstone grading dossiers from globally recognized gemological authorities including the Gemological Institute of America (GIA) and International Gemological Institute (IGI).

Because our pieces feature untreated and natural geological formations, minute microscopic growth signatures—such as three-phase inclusions (“jardins”) in Colombian emeralds or natural silk rutile in Kashmir-lineage sapphires—are inherent to authentic royal gemstones and do not constitute structural flaws.

All retail valuations are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) inclusive of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) unless otherwise explicitly specified during private invoice issuance.

We accept major credit instruments (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay), Net Banking channels, and Unified Payments Interface (UPI) flows.

“OLIVER does not store, harvest, or process payment card primary account numbers (PAN), CVVs, banking passwords, or UPI personal identification numbers on its internal cloud infrastructure. All financial data flows directly through end-to-end 256-bit encrypted channels supervised under the regulatory guidelines of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).”

In the event of an unexpected gateway drop or debit without order generation, banking reconciliation typically settles the funds back to your source account within 3 to 7 working banking days per standard RBI settlement rules.
