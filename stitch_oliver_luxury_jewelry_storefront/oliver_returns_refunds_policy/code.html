SECTION 01 OVERVIEW

Craft & Archival Custody Philosophy

At Oliver Stones, every piece is crafted with care, and we want you to love what you wear. This Refund & Return Policy outlines the terms under which returns, exchanges, and refunds are accepted.

verified ASSURANCE & SECURITY PROTOCOL

Every jewel is dispatched in secured protective packaging with serialized certification. To maintain chain-of-custody integrity, all return and exchange submissions undergo certified inspection upon arrival at our atelier.

SECTION 02 QUALIFICATION

Eligibility for Returns

To preserve the metallurgical hygiene and pristine value of our inventory, return requests are accepted strictly when the following conditions are met:

1

7-Day Window

Returns must be requested within 7 days of delivery.

2

Original Condition & Packaging

The product must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition with all tags, certificates (if any), and original packaging intact.

3

Custom & Made-to-Order Pieces

Customized, engraved, or made-to-order pieces are not eligible for return or exchange unless the item is defective or damaged.

4

Earrings Hygiene Clause

Earrings, for hygiene reasons, are strictly non-returnable unless defective.

SECTION 03 REQUEST INTAKE

How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, please email us at oliverstones.co@gmail.com with your order number, reason for return, and photos of the product (if applicable) within 7 days of delivery. Our team will review your request and share further instructions.

SECTION 04 REFUNDS

Refunds & Processing

Once your returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 7–10 business days. Shipping charges, if any, are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part.

payments REFUND DISBURSEMENT TIMELINE

Settlements are credited directly back to the original method of payment. You will receive an automated confirmation dispatch as soon as the banking clearance is executed.

SECTION 05 EXCHANGES

Exchanges

We are happy to offer exchanges for a different size or design of equal value, subject to availability, within 7 days of delivery. Any price difference will be adjusted accordingly.

SECTION 06 IN-TRANSIT CLAIMS

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos and your order number. We will arrange a replacement, repair, or full refund at no additional cost to you.

schedule Prompt documentation within 48 hours allows immediate activation of our courier transit insurance and expedited workshop slot.
SECTION 07 EXCLUSIONS

Non-Returnable Situations

Certain scenarios and product categories are non-returnable and classified as final sale:

close Items damaged due to misuse, negligence, or normal wear and tear
close Items returned without original packaging or after the 7-day window
close Sale or clearance items marked as final sale
close Customized, engraved, or bespoke made-to-order creations
close Earrings (hygiene policy), unless defective
SECTION 08 CANCELLATIONS

Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placement, provided they have not already been processed or dispatched. Please contact us immediately to request a cancellation.
