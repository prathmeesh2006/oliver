How Can We Help?

Frequently asked questions about our jewellery, orders, and customer care.

Our client team assists with certified gemstone origins, standard Indian and international ring sizing guides, and custom bespoke specifications. Inquire via our contact form above or reach us at Oliverstones.co@gmail.com.
Once your order is dispatched, a tracking dossier with armored transit credentials will be transmitted to your email. You can also monitor your active order through your Account Profile or by providing your Order ID to our concierge.
All orders enjoy complimentary armored courier dispatch across India, fully insured from our atelier to your doorstep with tamper-evident packaging and OTP-verified delivery. Read our full Shipping Policy for estimated transit timelines.
We offer an online exchange and return process for unworn heirlooms within their inspection window, subject to security seal verification. Please review our Returns & Exchange Policy or notify our client care team to initiate an insured pickup.
The OLIVER Privilege program unlocks private archival previews, priority bespoke allocations, and dedicated digital concierge routing. Inquire with our specialists or visit the Privilege Vault to learn more.
