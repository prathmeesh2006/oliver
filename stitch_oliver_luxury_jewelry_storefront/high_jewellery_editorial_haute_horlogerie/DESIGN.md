## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-luxury editorial maison aesthetic tailored for Indian luxury jewellery shoppers. The visual narrative combines quiet architectural restraint with deep heritage romanticism. Drawing inspiration from high-fashion print monographs and traditional couture houses, the interface prioritizes expansive gallery whitespace, surgical typographic calibration, and tactile material integrity over fast-fashion commercial clutter.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Archival:** High-fashion layouts that favor full-bleed portraits, asymmetric balancing, and precise grid alignments reminiscent of luxury periodicals.
- **Understated Opulence:** Replacing abrasive gold glitter and neon accents with disciplined warm cream backgrounds (`#F4EFDF`) grounded by regal deep garnets (`#710001` and `#710014`) and rich noir foundations.
- **Pristine Micro-Typography:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with airy, widely tracked modernist sans-serif labels in all-caps, conferring archival dignity to every price, specification, and action.
- **Sovereign Restraint:** Clean, unembellished borders, zero-radius geometry, and absence of heavy drop shadows or glossy skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is strictly anchored in the maison’s signature warm vellum cream and deep royal garnets, complemented by neutral archival charcoal and bronze accents.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary (`#710001` - Garnet Deep):** Used for authoritative moments—brand identity mark, high-priority interactive buttons, active drawer accents, and category title emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#710014` - Garnet Wine):** A richer, cooler wine tone applied to hover states, secondary highlights, privileged badge outlines, and focused UI trims.
- **Neutral (`#F4EFDF` - Vellum Cream):** The foundational canvas. Replaces cold digital whites with an authentic warm, tactile linen-parchment tone that softens gemstone reflection.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` - Bronze Foil):** Subtle muted metallic tone reserved strictly for the Privilege membership emblem, subtle rules, and fine jewel metadata.
- **Charcoal Ink (`#121212`):** Body typography, technical specs, and high-legibility Indian Rupee (`₹`) pricing.

### Functional States
- **Hairline Dividers:** `rgba(113, 0, 1, 0.14)` preserves strict structural demarcation without interrupting spatial flow.
- **Surface Elevation:** Never uses artificial gray shadows; depth is rendered using stacked layers of `#F4EFDF`, `#FAF7F0`, and crisp hairline boundaries.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on the dynamic interplay between classical editorial serif forms (**EB Garamond**) and crisp, rhythmic geometric sans-serif modernism (**Montserrat**).

### Typographic Rules
- **Editorial Serifs (Headlines & Editorial Callouts):** Set with generous line heights and tight negative tracking in larger scales. Garamond imparts museum-grade stature to collection titles, hero narratives, and section launches.
- **Modernist Sans-Serif (Body & Micro-Metrics):** Body prose uses lightweight Montserrat (`300`) to guarantee high legibility across mobile displays without feeling dense.
- **All-Caps Tracking:** All metadata, badges, category navigation items, button texts, and price markers must be set in uppercase with tracked letterspacing (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) to project architectural deliberation.
- **Price Presentation:** Indian Rupee currency tokens (`₹`) are rendered using medium-weight Montserrat to balance seamlessly with accompanying figures (e.g., `₹14,900`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an architectural 12-column responsive grid anchored by generous outer margins and disciplined rhythmic pauses.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1440px maximum container width; 64px (`4rem`) outer margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Used for alternating visual cadences (e.g., 7-column portrait alongside a 5-column editorial text block).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 768px–1024px; 32px (`2rem`) margins and 16px (`1rem`) gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Below 768px; 20px (`1.25rem`) safe outer margins with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters.
- **Macro Whitespace:** Sections must breathe with vertical separation of `space-xl` (mobile) to `space-2xl` (desktop), creating a deliberate cadence that prevents product crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft diffuse blurs, neon drop shadows, and heavy floating tiers. Depth is achieved purely through deliberate layering, hairline borders, and atmospheric scrims.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4EFDF` creates the warm foundational vellum.
- **Tonal Layers:** Product showcases and elevated modules sit on `#FAF7F0` surfaces, framed by `1px solid rgba(113, 0, 1, 0.14)` hairline perimeter lines.
- **Drawers & Modals (Cart, Filters, Navigation):** Slid horizontally from viewport edges over a 60% opacity dark scrim (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.6)`). The sliding drawer maintains a crisp `#F4EFDF` surface with a sheer left border, entirely free from box-shadow gradients.
- **Hover Transitions:** Product imagery subtle scale transition (1.02x zoom over 600ms ease-out) within sharp overflow-hidden frames, signaling luxury craft through interaction timing rather than spatial lifting.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary is uncompromisingly sharp (`0px` border-radius).

### Geometric Rationale
- **Razor Architecture:** All buttons, input containers, product cards, toast notifications, badges, and modals have precise 90-degree corners.
- **Editorial Alignment:** Sharp geometry mirrors traditional physical lookbooks and cut-glass showcases, creating seamless alignment between typography, borders, and photographic edges.

## Components

### 1. Announcement Bar
- **Dimensions & Style:** 36px fixed height; `#710001` solid background with pure `#F4EFDF` label-micro typography.
- **Behavior:** Subtle text rotation or singular curated messaging (e.g., "COMPLIMENTARY INSURED SHIPPING ACROSS INDIA"). Center aligned with 0.22em tracking.

### 2. Luxury Header & Navigation
- **Structure:** Centered architectural OLIVER logo mark; split navigation categories on desktop (`SHOP`, `RINGS`, `NECKLACES`, `PENDANTS`, `EARRINGS`, `SETS`, `PRIVILEGE`).
- **Right Utilities:** Search, Wishlist, Account, and Bag counters displayed as refined stroke icons with numerical counters in tracked micro-type.
- **Header State:** Sticky header transitions from transparent to solid `#F4EFDF` with a hairline bottom border upon scrolling.

### 3. Product Cards
- **Composition:** 4:5 vertical photographic ratio. Minimal visual friction.
- **Metadata Layout:** Image resting on flush background; subtle top-right floating wishlist outline; product title in `body-sm` (uppercase); gemstone/material subtitle in muted charcoal; price displayed with prominent `₹` symbol.
- **Privilege Callout:** Member pricing displayed beneath standard retail price in garnet text: "₹14,155 PRIVILEGE PRICE".

### 4. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button:** `#710001` solid background, `#F4EFDF` text, 0px radius, 52px height, all-caps `label-uppercase`. On hover: `#710014` with a smooth 250ms transition.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** 1px hairline border in `#710001`, transparent background, `#710001` text. On hover: `#710001` background with `#F4EFDF` text.
- **Text Link Button:** Tracked uppercase label with an active underline offset by 4px.

### 5. OLIVER Privilege Card & Badge
- **Privilege Badge:** Framed by a 1px border in `#C5A880`, containing `#710001` typography on `#FAF7F0` surface.
- **Privilege Plan Card:** Dedicated hero block featuring a subtle bronze gradient rule, clear disclosure of ₹149/month recurring commitment, and 5% / 10% birthday discount mechanics.

### 6. Cart Drawer & Checkout Trims
- **Slide Drawer:** Right-docked, 440px desktop width, 100vw mobile width. Hairline section dividers separating line items.
- **Pricing Breakdown:** Subtotal, verified membership deduction, and shipping calculated clearly with INR currency formatting. Includes a sticky checkout CTA at the bottom viewport edge.

### 7. Form Fields & Input Controls
- **Inputs:** Minimalist underline style or full 1px hairline enclosure. `#121212` text on `#F4EFDF` ground; floating uppercase labels.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts from hairline garnet to solid `#710001` without generic browser halos.

### 8. Accordions (Product Details & FAQs)
- **Style:** Border-top and border-bottom hairline dividers only; crisp serif headline or tracked sans-serif title with a thin `+` / `−` icon transition.
