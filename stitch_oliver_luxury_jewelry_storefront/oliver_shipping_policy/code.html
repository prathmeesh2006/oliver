Introduction & Agreement

This Shipping Policy explains how Oliver Stones processes, packages, and delivers orders placed through our website or official channels. By placing an order with us, you agree to the terms outlined below.

Section 02

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will begin processing on the next business day.

schedule HIGH-DEMAND & FESTIVE PERIODS

During high-demand periods (festive sales, Rakhi gifting season, or new launches), processing may take up to 5 business days. We will notify you of any significant delays via email or phone.

Section 04

Shipping Charges

calculate

Transparent Checkout Calculation

Shipping charges, if applicable, are calculated at checkout based on order value, weight, and delivery location. We may offer free shipping promotions from time to time, which will be clearly indicated on the website.

Section 05

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number and courier details via email or SMS. You can track your shipment directly through the courier partner's website.

Section 06

Packaging

shield PROTECTIVE ARCHIVAL CASING

Every Oliver Stones piece is carefully inspected and packaged in branded, protective packaging designed to preserve the integrity of the jewelry during transit. Please retain original packaging until you have inspected the product.

Section 07

Delayed, Lost or Damaged Shipments

If your order has not arrived within the expected timeline, or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery (or expected delivery date) at oliverstones.co@gmail.com with your order number and relevant photos.

We will work with our courier partner to investigate and resolve the issue, including replacement or refund where applicable.
Section 08

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address, pin code, and contact number are accurate at the time of order. Oliver Stones is not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by incorrect or incomplete address details provided by the customer.
