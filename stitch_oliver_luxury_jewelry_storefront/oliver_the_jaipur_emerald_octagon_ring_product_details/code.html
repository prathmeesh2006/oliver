The Geometry of Solitude

Mined from the venerable Muzo reserves in the Andean foothills, each emerald selected for The Jaipur Octagon embodies the intense foliage hues revered by Rajput royalty. Set flush within a substantial modern step-bezel, it combines ancient stone veneration with modern architectural geometry.

MAISON ARCHIVE · JAIPUR ATELIER 1974

Conceived as a talisman of stillness, The Jaipur Emerald Octagon Ring departs from standard prong configurations in favor of an uninterrupted solid 18K yellow gold lip. This protective bezel encases the emerald's natural crystalline edges while accentuating its vivid chroma.

Each octagon emerald is hand-selected by our master gemologist in Johari Bazaar, prioritizing natural inner gardens ("jardin") that confirm unheated origin and untreated provenance.
